# Ability Scores

#### Standard Array

The standard array consists of six values: 15, 14, 13, 12, 10, 8. These are assigned to the six abilities as desired, then modified by race. 

{: .note}
> ##### Example: High Elf Wizard
> 
> | Ability      | Value | Race Bonus | Total |
> | :----------- | :---- | :--------- | :---- |
> | Strength     | 10    | +0         | 10    |
> | Dexterity    | 14    | +2         | 16    |
> | Constitution | 12    | +0         | 12    |
> | Intelligence | 15    | +1         | 16    |
> | Wisdom       | 08    | +0         | 08    |
> | Charisma     | 13    | +0         | 13    |
