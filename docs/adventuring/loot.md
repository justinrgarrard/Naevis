# Loot

{: .mundane-item}
> **Loot (10 gp)**
>
> *Treasure, 5 lb.*
>
> Carefully preserved monster viscera, rocks that glow when exposed to moonlight, damaged armor made of quality materials; this is anything that might be of interest to a curious merchant.

> ##### Value by Weight
> Most loot is represented as a generic object with its value determined by weight. [Magic Items](magic_items) are the exception.
>
> ##### Encumbrance
> Stronger adventurers can carry more loot out of a dungeon. Review the rules under [weight](../more/review/weight).
>
> ##### Containers
> Unless you plan on storing loot next to your rations, you'll want to purchase some [pouches](../character_creation/equipment/tools). Remember that backpacks have a maximum capacity of 30 lbs.
