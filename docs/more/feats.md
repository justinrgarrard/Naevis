# Feats and Multiclassing

{: .note}
> ##### Cross-Disciplinary Training
> Feats allow a character to gain benefits outside of their class, such as spellcasting or equipment proficiencies.
>
> ##### Multiclassing
> The requirements on this page replace the multiclassing rules found in the [System Reference Document](../SRD_CC_v5.1.pdf).

---

{: .feat}
> ##### Multiclass: Cleric
>
> *Requirements: Wisdom 13, Not a Cleric*
> 
> You learn three Cleric cantrips. When you next level up, you can choose to gain a level in Cleric. 

{: .feat}
> ##### Multiclass: Fighter
>
> *Requirements: Strength 13, Not a Fighter*
> 
> You gain a Fighting Style and one of the following:
> 
> * Proficiency in shields
> * Proficiency in armor
> * Proficiency in two weapons
> 
> The armor proficiency, should you choose it, is the lightest armor you are not currently proficient in. When you next level up, you can choose to gain a level in Fighter.

{: .feat}
> ##### Multiclass: Rogue
>
> *Requirements: Dexterity 13, Not a Rogue*
> 
> You gain proficiency in one skill and one of the following:
>
> * Proficiency in Cant
> * Proficiency with Thieves' Tools
> * Expertise in one skill
>
> When you next level up, you can choose to gain a level in Rogue. 

{: .feat}
> ##### Multiclass: Wizard
>
> *Requirements: Intelligence 13, Not a Wizard*
> 
> You learn three Wizard cantrips. When you next level up, you can choose to gain a level in Wizard.
