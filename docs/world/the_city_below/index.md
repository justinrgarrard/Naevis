# The City Below

> ##### The City Above
> 
> There is little to be seen of the City surface-side. No carvings, no burial grounds or temples, nothing at all to suggest what sort of place it may once have been. The remnants are piecemeal and shattered.

> ##### The City Below

> Crude tunnels can be found in the basements and collapsed courtyards of the City Above. They wind in a maddening labyrinth that defies modern cartography.

> ##### Language
>
> City Dwellers share the same spoken [language](../../adventuring/languages#undercommon) (Undercommon). Magical practitioners also use a written language (Infernal). They are not proficient in Common. 
>
> ##### Magic
>
> City Dwellers do not use magic as Kindred do. They are neither wizards nor clerics, instead practicing entirely distinct traditions of spellcraft.
>
> ##### Technology
>
> While inhabitants of the City tend to have stronger magic than Kindred, their technological prowess is considerably weaker. Weapons are made of stone and bone, clothing is made of monster hides, and worked metal is a rarity.
>
> ##### Architecture
>
> Buildings are uncommon in the City Below. Instead, elaborate tents and decorated caves make up the most common living quarters. Most are charmed with exotic forms of warding to sound alarms or rebuke trespassers.
>
> ##### Religion
>
> Denizens of the City worship the [Nine Devil Princes](devil_princes), a pantheon of mythical gods. Where the deities of Kindred are held to exist outside of the physical world, Devil Princes are rumored to walk among their followers in the flesh.
