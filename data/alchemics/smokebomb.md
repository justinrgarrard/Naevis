# Smoke Bomb

*Grenade, common, illicit*

As an action, you can throw this ball up to 20 feet from you. Upon impact it explodes into a 10-foot radius sphere of smoke.

The sphere spreads around corners, and its area is heavily obscured. It lasts for one minute or until a wind of moderate or greater speed (at least 10 miles per hour) disperses it. If the user has proficiency in _Tinker's Tools_ or _Mason Tools_, the duration increases by a number of minutes equal to their Intelligence bonus.
