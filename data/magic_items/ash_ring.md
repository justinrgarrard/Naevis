# Ash Ring

*Spellcasting focus, common, requires attunement*

A black wooden ring that radiates a soft warmth.

While attuned to this ring and wearing it, you can cast the [*Fire Bolt*](../srd_spells/shocking_grasp) cantrip using Charisma as your spellcasting modifier.
