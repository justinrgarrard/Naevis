# Cleric: Stone Legionnaire

_Warrior monks that seek enlightenment through adversity. The Stone Legionnaires were originally a Dwarven fraternity of ascetic priests._


> ##### Stone Domain Spells
> 
> | Cleric Level |                               Spells |
> | :----------- | -----------------------------------: |
> | 1st          |                      Command, Shield |
> | 3rd          |            Arcane Lock, Magic Weapon |
> | 5th          | Counterspell, Protection From Energy |
> | 7th          |                Death Ward, Stoneskin |
> | 9th          |              Passwall, Wall of Stone |
> 
> 
> ##### Bonus Proficiency
> When you choose this domain at 1st level, you gain proficiency with heavy armor.
> 
> 
> ##### Stone Sword
> At 1st level, any non-magical weapon you wield counts as magical while you wield it, gaining a +1 to attack and damage rolls.
> 
> 
> ##### Channel Divinity: Lesser Stoneskin
> Starting at 2nd level, you can use your Channel Divinity to protect yourself against harm.
> 
> You can expend a bonus action to gain resistance against nonmagical piercing, slashing, or bludgeoning damage. The damage type must be > chosen when the feature is used. The effect lasts a number of rounds equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum +1).
> 
> 
> ##### Qi Weapon
> Beginning at 6th level, your gain the ability to imbue qi into weapons. When you make a weapon attack, you can add your Wisdom modifier > to the damage roll.
> 
> 
> ##### Divine Strike
> At 8th level, you gain the ability to infuse your weapon strikes with divine energy. Once on each of your turns when you hit a creature > with a weapon attack, you can cause the attack to deal an extra 1d8 damage of the weapon's type to the target. When you reach 14th > level, the extra damage increases to 2d8.
> 
> 
> ##### Inner Strength
> At 17th level, you gain an additional use of _Channel Divinity_.
